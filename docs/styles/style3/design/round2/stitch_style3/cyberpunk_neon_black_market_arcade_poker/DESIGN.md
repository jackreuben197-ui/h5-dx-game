## Brand & Style
This design system embodies an underground, high-stakes cybernetic arcade terminal. Blending heavy industrial mecha aesthetics with high-octane neon hardware, the visual atmosphere evokes an illicit rainy night market deck hidden deep within a mega-city underbelly. 

Visual pillars balance mechanical industrialism with luminous electro-luminescence:
- **Mecha Industrial Armor:** Heavy chamfered chassis paneling, reinforced structural bezels, exposed bolts, conduit wire channels, and hazard warning geometry.
- **Split-Channel Neon Ambience:** High-voltage electric cyan lightguide rails on the left flank converging against searing hot magenta/pink arrays on the right flank, washed over a deep dark-violet rainswept skyline.
- **Tactile Physicality:** Chunky physical arcade switchgear, 3D beveled surfaces, mechanical actuation states, and illuminated edge piping.
- **Table Legibility:** High-contrast felt isolation using deep tactical purple with subtle precision coordinate grids to keep card gameplay and wager metrics crystal clear amidst the surrounding hardware chaos.

## Colors
The palette operates on high-voltage phosphors cut through an abyssal industrial baseline:

- **Primary (`#00f0ff` - Electric Cyan):** Drives core interactive lightguide conduits, left-side rail illumination, call/check actions, active chip glow, and digital heads-up data.
- **Secondary (`#ff0055` - Hot Magenta):** Powers the right-side overdriven neon array, rival threat indicators, showdown highlights, high-roller pots, and critical alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#ffe600` - Acid Warning Yellow):** Applied to hazard banding, raise multipliers, aggressive wager buttons, and industrial warning stencils.
- **Neutral (`#120d1f` - Void Purple / Carbon Chassis):** Provides the deep substrate for the poker table felt, structural mecha armor frames, and carbonized bevels.

### Functional Palette Applications
- **Felt Play Area:** Multi-stop radial tone from `#1c1230` to `#0d0818` with 8% opacity cyan/violet coordinate lines.
- **Card Substrates:** Stark `#fbfcf9` Ivory finish with stark `#d91438` vermilion hearts/diamonds and pitch-black `#08070b` spades/clubs to preserve instantaneous recognizability during speed runs.
- **Chassis Metal:** Matte carbon plates (`#1a1924`) bounded by etched zinc borders (`#2f2d3d`) and deep hydraulic shadow wells (`#07050d`).

## Typography
Typographic treatment follows a strict cyber-tactical division of labor:

- **Display & Main Headers (`Space Grotesk`):** Built with mechanical precision, angular geometric terminals, and uppercase tracking. Conveys heavy equipment badges, table designations, and critical showdown banners.
- **Narrative & System Copy (`Chivo`):** High-clarity grotesque providing instantaneous comprehension under dark, noisy game conditions without distracting from betting rhythms.
- **Telemetry, Stacks & Timers (`JetBrains Mono`):** Zero-ambiguity monospaced digital figures. Every chip count, blind rate, pot calculation, and card identifier aligns on an exact character grid with localized phosphor glow filters.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a rigid, arcade-chassis encapsulation model:

- **The Cabinet Viewport:** The outer layout represents the structural bezel of the arcade cabinet. Desktop views anchor the central table within a 12-column viewport locked with heavy mechanical borders. Mobile layouts collapse side modules into bottom-mounted cockpit drawers.
- **Table Centerpiece:** An encapsulated oval/lozenge felt core with dedicated seating nodes spaced along geometric vectors. Seating pods enforce fixed bounds using `space-md` gaps so chip stacks, player tags, and hole cards never overlap betting paths.
- **Action Dashboard Dock:** Locked to the bottom third of the display. Control buttons, sliders, and telemetry operate within a continuous, armored console bar utilizing strict `space-sm` and `space-md` component spacing.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is constructed through physical mecha plating, beveled industrial relief, and targeted optical neon blooms rather than diffuse generic drop shadows:

- **Layer 0 (Atmosphere):** Dark violet city backdrop with diagonal 24-degree rain streaks, drenched in distant neon skyline scatter.
- **Layer 1 (Cabinet Armor Frame):** Dark matte carbon and gunmetal plates (`#120f1c` / `#1e1a2b`) detailed with inset panel gaps, hex-bolt rivets (`#3d3952`), and hazard chevron decals.
- **Layer 2 (The Table Felt):** Recessed beneath the armor lip via an inner cast shadow (`inset 0 4px 18px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`), layered with a faint purple micro-grid pattern.
- **Layer 3 (Hardware Modules & Card Wells):** Player seats and pot trays sit as beveled fixtures extruded above the felt with dual-tone edge highlights: `#00f0ff` rim lighting across the left facets and `#ff0055` edge catch across the right.
- **Layer 4 (Physical Buttons & Cards):** Deep 3D beveled tactile buttons with heavy mechanical push states (`transform: translateY(4px)` with bottom rim reduction) and 3D playing cards casting sharp, directional ground occlusions (`0 8px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`).
- **Layer 5 (Lightguides & Overdrive Glow):** Neon rail channels emit high-intensity double-pass blurs (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px #00f0ff, 0 0 32px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` and corresponding `#ff0055` passes).

## Shapes
The design system enforces a **Chamfered & Angular (Sharp)** structural language:

- **Chamfered Geometries:** Standard corners employ 45-degree cut corners (chamfers of 4px, 8px, and 16px via `clip-path: polygon(...)`) rather than radial roundedness, evoking machined armor plating and arcade hardware panels.
- **Card Silhouettes:** Playing cards use precise 4px micro-radii to balance industrial arcade styling with familiar card deck handling.
- **Slanted Dividers:** Warning stripes and chassis partitions are canted at a signature 45-degree or 60-degree angle to reinforce dynamic mechanical tension.

## Components

### Arcade Actuator Buttons (Action Console)
- **Fold Button:** Graphite matte base (`#1d1b24`), heavy bottom bevel (`#0a080f`), stark cool-grey stencil typography (`#9f9ba8`), subtle red underglow on hover.
- **Call / Check Button:** Electric Cyan chassis (`#00f0ff`), deep metallic rim (`#008b94`), black bold labels (`#031012`), intense cyan outer lightguide glow (`0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6)`).
- **Raise / All-In Button:** Acid Hazard Yellow chassis (`#ffe600`), blackened contrast lettering (`#121000`), angled black hazard stripes flanking the button edges, amber/gold flash on activation.
- **Tactile Physics:** 4px mechanical bottom lip that collapses into the table panel when pressed, triggering instantaneous click audio-visual feedback.

### Playing Cards
- **Faces:** High-legibility crisp ivory (`#fbfcf9`) substrate, ultra-crisp indices using custom high-contrast red (`#d91438`) and ink black (`#0a080f`).
- **Backs:** Dark obsidian field bordered by twin high-intensity electric cyan and magenta copper-circuit traces meeting at an underground black-market cipher seal.

### Digital Stack Tags & Telemetry Chips
- **Player HUD Panels:** Monolithic chamfered badges with dark translucent cores (`rgba(18, 13, 31, 0.85)`), bordered by 1px metallic bevels and illuminated neon status bars.
- **Stack Readout:** JetBrains Mono figures rendered in high-brightness phosphors with dynamic warning states (turns Acid Yellow under low-stack jeopardy).
- **Pot Indicator:** Overhead floating counter encased in a dual-color bracket (`[ CYAN | MAGENTA ]`) anchored over the table felt.

### Pot Sliders & Betting Toggles
- **Track:** Recessed conduit trench with exposed wire textures running beneath a glowing cyan fill bar.
- **Thumb:** Chunky, grooved mecha thumb-switch with side hazard chevrons and a live floating tooltip tag projecting the exact bet value in real-time.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Switches
- **Toggles:** Heavy industrial rocker switches that illuminate an internal LED (Cyan for active, Dim Amber for standby) when engaged.
- **Checkboxes:** Chamfered square metal housings with an animated diagonal neon slash (`/`) upon check.
